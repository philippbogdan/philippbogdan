# Philipp Bogdan

I point agents at open scientific problems and judge what comes back. Most of it does not survive my review.

- [torux](https://github.com/philippbogdan/torux): a stellarator's coils in, its vacuum flux surface out, at max |B·n|/|B| of 10⁻¹⁵ in a median 4.2 s. A Proxima Fusion scientist [wrote](https://github.com/philippbogdan/torux/issues/1) that it "verifies vacuum surfaces at 1e-15 in about a minute, which none of our field-line or Boozer-surface routes come close to".
- [Zero-bulk metamaterials](https://github.com/philippbogdan/zero-bulk-metamaterials): the closest anyone has come to settling [Graeme Milton's open question](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.03394): a material that squeezes freely yet twists as stiffly as physics allows.
- [Proxima Fusion's VMEC++ #819](https://github.com/proximafusion/vmecpp/pull/819) (merged): 16 to 100x lower interpolation error in its vacuum-field tables.
- [MACE #1806](https://github.com/ACEsuit/mace/pull/1806) (merged): magnetic MACE's energy made continuous at the cutoff.

## Papers

- [Learner history and calibration](https://openreview.net/forum?id=9uPqgPvXtJ): practice histories improved forecasts of pupils' maths performance (Brier score 0.2148 to 0.2027). **NeurIPS 2026 MATH-AI workshop paper, poster.**
- [Atom Learning Model (ALM)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21106): the learning model behind a maths tutor used by 373 pupils for seven weeks, 26k+ marked attempts. arXiv.
- [INSTRUMENTAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15905): recovers 28 synth settings to recreate a sound from a song. [Code](https://github.com/philippbogdan/instrumental), [live demo](https://philippbogdan.com/instrumental). arXiv.
- [Optimal-transport cartogram](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14089): population maps without rotation versus diffusion's 7 to 9 degrees median on matched tests. [Code](https://github.com/philippbogdan/historical-cartogram). arXiv.

# Philipp Bogdan

Curious across domains and build systems that just work.

- [torux](https://github.com/philippbogdan/torux): your default vacuum solver. 10⁻¹⁵ leak, 4.2 s median solve, any coil.
- [Zero-bulk metamaterials](https://github.com/philippbogdan/zero-bulk-metamaterials): the closest anyone has come to settling [Graeme Milton's open question](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.03394): a material that squeezes freely yet twists as stiffly as physics allows. **Est. value: [$0.1-3M](https://github.com/philippbogdan/philippbogdan/blob/main/values/zero-bulk-metamaterials.md)**
- [Proxima Fusion's VMEC++ #819](https://github.com/proximafusion/vmecpp/pull/819) (merged): made the magnetic field in their fusion design code 16 to 100 times more accurate. **Est. value: [$0.05-1M](https://github.com/philippbogdan/philippbogdan/blob/main/values/vmecpp-819.md)**
- [MACE #1806](https://github.com/ACEsuit/mace/pull/1806) (merged): with two people already investigating it, took an energy jump out of the magnetic models of a widely used machine-learned force field: up to 1 eV on freshly initialised models, now at rounding error.
- [Tokamak Energy's GSFit #127](https://github.com/tokamak-energy/gsfit/pull/127) (open): works out the shape of their plasma up to 2.9 times faster.

## Papers

- [Learner history and calibration](https://openreview.net/forum?id=9uPqgPvXtJ): practice histories improved forecasts of pupils' maths performance (Brier score 0.2148 to 0.2027). **NeurIPS 2026 MATH-AI workshop paper, poster.**
- [Atom Learning Model (ALM)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21106): maths tutor; 373 pupils, 26k+ marked attempts in seven weeks. arXiv.
- [INSTRUMENTAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15905): recovers 28 synth settings to recreate a sound from a song. [Code](https://github.com/philippbogdan/instrumental), [live demo](https://philippbogdan.com/instrumental). arXiv.
- [Optimal-transport cartogram](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14089): population maps without rotation versus diffusion's 7 to 9 degrees median on matched tests. [Code](https://github.com/philippbogdan/historical-cartogram). arXiv.

My calculation assumes a **20% to 40% chance of useful industrial uptake**. The weekly value is a rounded US employment-cost proxy, including benefits and overhead. The [experimental limitations](https://github.com/philippbogdan/one-step-turbulence/blob/main/REPRODUCE.md#development-scope) constrain the upside. The demonstrated setting is two-dimensional periodic flow, with missing spatial structures and large temporal correlation errors. The [5,360-fold cost estimate](https://github.com/philippbogdan/one-step-turbulence/blob/main/REPRODUCE.md#cost-estimates) extrapolates statistical uncertainty, excludes training and establishes no saving at equal physical accuracy. I therefore assign this work's value mainly to reusable research infrastructure and better evaluation decisions. Substantial production savings would require further validation and development; actual uptake could be zero.
